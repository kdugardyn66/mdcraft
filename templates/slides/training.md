## Exercise 1 · 10 minutes

> [!TIP]
> Take an e-mail you sent this week. Rewrite the first two sentences so that a reader who stops there knows **what you want** and **by when**.

Then swap with your neighbour and give one piece of feedback.

<!-- slide class="quote" -->

> If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough.
>
> — attributed to many people

<!-- slide -->

## Quick quiz

1. Where does the main message belong? *At the start.*
2. How long should most sentences be? *Under 20 words.*
3. What helps skimmers most? *Clear headings and short lists.*

<!-- slide -->

## Recap

- Lead with the point
- One idea per sentence
- Cut every word that doesn't work
- Help the skimmer with structure
