## Purpose

This memo explains the updated hybrid working policy that takes effect on **1 January {{next_year}}** and what you need to do before then.

## What changes

- Teams agree on **two anchor days** a week when everyone is in the office.
- The remaining days can be worked from home or from any of our offices.
- The home-office allowance rises from €30 to **€45 a month**.
- Meeting rooms are booked through the new room planner; open desks stay first come, first served.

> [!NOTE]
> Roles that need to be on site every day (reception, facilities, the lab) keep their current schedules.

## Why

The staff survey in September showed that 78 % of you want more flexibility, while 64 % also miss spontaneous contact with colleagues. Anchor days give us both: time together when it matters and quiet focus time at home.

## What you need to do

- [ ] Agree on your team's anchor days with your manager by **15 December**.
- [ ] Update your working pattern in the HR portal.
- [ ] Read the full policy on the intranet (link below).

## Questions

Your manager or the People & Culture team will gladly answer your questions. We will also hold two open sessions in the canteen on 4 and 11 December at 12:30.
