## Version 5 is out

After nine months of work, version 5 shipped on the 3rd. It brings the most requested feature of the last two years — offline mode — plus a faster search and a fresh look. Early numbers are encouraging: daily active users rose 14 % in the first week, and the app store rating went up from 4.3 to 4.6.

> “We finally have something we can use on the train.”

A big thank-you to everyone who tested the beta and sent us 1,200 pieces of feedback.

### Hello, Lisbon

Our new office in Lisbon opens on 1 December with a team of eight engineers and two customer-success managers. It puts us closer to our customers in southern Europe and gives us a sunny place for the next team offsite. Want to spend a few weeks there? Ask your manager about the exchange programme.

### Meet the support team

They answered 9,400 questions last quarter, in five languages, with an average satisfaction of 9.1. This month we sat down with Inês, Karim and Femke to talk about the strangest support request ever (it involved a goat) and how they stay calm on launch days.

### Green commute challenge

From 1 to 30 November, log your bike, train and walking trips. The team with the most green kilometres wins a dinner — and for every 1,000 km we plant ten trees.

### From the kitchen

The new coffee machine on the second floor makes oat-milk flat whites. Please be kind to it: rinse the steam wand.
